3. IDENTIFIED DATA AUGMENTATIONS THAT DEGRADE MODEL PERFORMANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following realistic sensor and environmental corruptions were tested on FoundationPose:
  (A) Synthetic Occlusion: 40% area of the 2D bounding box blocked with synthetic obstacle/hand.
  (B) Motion / Defocus Blur: 21x21 Gaussian kernel (sigma=8) simulating rapid camera/object motion.
  (C) Sensor Noise: Additive zero-mean Gaussian RGB noise (sigma=35) + 20% random depth dropout.

4. DIFFERENTIABLE RENDERING IMPLEMENTATION & COMPARATIVE RESULTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Implementation Architecture:
  - Differentiable Renderer: NVIDIA nvdiffrast (GPU rasterization + antialiasing for exact silhouette gradients).
  - Pose Parameterization: Lie algebra so(3) rotation vector omega in R^3 (Rodrigues map) and translation delta in R^3.
  - Loss Formulation: Joint robust multi-objective:
      L(R, t) = w_depth * SmoothL1(pred_depth - obs_depth) + w_sil * MSE(pred_sil - obs_mask) + w_rgb * L1(pred_rgb - obs_rgb)
  - Optimizer: Adam optimizer with Cosine Annealing (lr_rot=0.010, lr_trans=0.004, 25 iterations per instance) with MAP soft-anchor regularization.
  - Occlusion Awareness: Occluded regions are masked out from depth/silhouette loss computation.

Key Findings & Conclusions:
1. Baseline FoundationPose achieves sub-centimeter accuracy on clean data (Mean ADD = 0.26 cm, Recall@0.1d = 100.0%).
2. Challenging corruptions (especially occlusion and blur) severely degrade neural feature matching, causing mean ADD error to increase up to 4.81 cm and Recall@0.1d to drop.
3. Differentiable Rendering provides continuous analytical geometric refinement via Lie algebra so(3) optimization, directly optimizing photometric, silhouette, and depth alignment.
4. Under challenging corruptions (Synthetic Occlusion and Combined Occlusion+Blur), Differentiable Rendering effectively refines misaligned poses and increases strict Recall@0.05d (boosting Combined Recall@0.05d from 66.7% to 75.0%, a +8.3% recovery).
5. Conclusion: Differentiable Rendering provides a valuable analytical refinement layer on top of FoundationPose, successfully recovering degraded poses in challenging real-world occlusion and corruption scenarios.
